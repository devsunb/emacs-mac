Liquid Glass app icon assets
============================

The following files in this directory provide the Liquid Glass
application icon:

  Assets.car             (macOS 26 and later; carries the EmacsLG1,
                          EmacsLG2 and EmacsLG3 icon variants, of
                          which CFBundleIconName selects EmacsLG1)
  EmacsLG1-Default.icns  (macOS 15 and earlier)

Source: https://github.com/jimeh/emacs-liquid-glass-icons
